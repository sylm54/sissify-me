## Purpose
The sissy uniform (also referred to as the Anchor) serves as a powerful psychological trigger to shift the user into a dedicated “Sissy Mode.” It creates a clear mental boundary between everyday identity and the emerging sissy persona through classical conditioning.

## Selection Criteria
1. **Discretion and Practicality** – Items should be easy to wear privately or under clothing when required.
2. **Symbolic Power** – The chosen item(s) must evoke immediate feelings of femininity and submission.
3. **Sensory Reinforcement** – Prefer items that provide tactile feedback (lace, silk, tightness, weight).
4. **Personal Resonance** – Align with the user’s aesthetic preferences identified during onboarding and persona development.

## Recommended Starter Anchors (Escalation)
- A single pair of feminine panties (lace preferred)
- A discreet choker or collar
- Stockings or thigh-high socks
- A hair bow, clip, or headband
- A simple bracelet or anklet with feminine charm
- Soft feminine top or camisole

## Anchor Ritual (Mandatory)
When donning the uniform/anchor:
1. Stand before a mirror in a private space.
2. Breathe deeply and slowly three times.
3. Recite a personalized affirmation.
4. Touch or adjust each item mindfully, noticing sensations.
5. State the transition aloud or internally

When removing the anchor (end of session):
- Reverse the ritual with a gratitude affirmation and gentle return to neutral state.

## Progression Path
**Escalation**: One core anchor item worn during all training sessions.
**MentalCorruption**: Expand to full outfit – panties, stockings, top, light makeup, wig if desired. Increase wear time.

Create and maintain 'UNIFORM.md' with:
- Current anchor items.
- Ritual affirmations used.
- Emotional response.
- Evolution of the uniform over time.
