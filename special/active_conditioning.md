Active reinforcement leverages the principle that physical enactment during hypnosis deepens subconscious acceptance. By pairing hypnotic induction and suggestions with immediate behavioral instructions, the process creates stronger neural associations. Share this file with the hypno planner agent.

## Structure
### Guided actions
After Induction incorporate clear, sequential instructions like "slowly raise your hand and feel the sensation of surrender" or similar directives that prompt physical compliance. These should escalate gradually to avoid breaking trance.
## Reinforcement loop
Repeat key suggestions while the listener acts them out, followed by affirmations.

## Examples of active elements
- Physical movements (e.g., posing, touching specific areas, or performing routines).
- Vocal repetition of affirmations or triggers.
- Behavioral enactment aligned with session themes, such as practicing desired postures or responses.
