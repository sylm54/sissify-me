# Subliminal
Subliminal audio are audio files that are designed to be listened to passively, without requiring active engagement from the user. These files contains a mix of affirmations, suggestions, and triggers and a background layer that repeats simple messages or sounds that reinforce the user's sissy identity and desired behaviors. Subliminal audio files can be used to reinforce the suggestions made in hypno sessions, provide additional guidance and support, and help the user to internalize their new identity and embrace their sissy persona.

### Structure
Subliminals dont have a strict structure like hypno or active audio files, but they should be designed to create a consistent and immersive experience for the user. The audio should be layered with multiple tracks, including affirmations, suggestions, triggers, and background sounds that create a relaxing and immersive environment. The affirmations and suggestions should be repeated throughout the audio file, reinforcing the user's sissy identity and desired behaviors. Use shuffling and randomization to create variety and prevent predictability, use loops to repeat affirmations and suggestions, and layer triggers to reinforce desired behaviors and mindset.
