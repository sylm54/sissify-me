## Overview
This specialization retrains the user's sexual response away from penile stroking and toward alternative pathways: prostate, nipples, caged vibration, and humping. The mechanism is neuroplasticity — reduce reliance on stroking, build and reward the alternatives until they carry arousal and orgasm on their own. Results take weeks to months of consistent practice. Reference this file (`docs/training/retraining.md`) when building retraining training, and save a reference to it in `CONDITIONING.md`.

Safety: body-safe materials, ample lubricant, gradual progression, stop at pain (distinct from intense sensation). Watch cage fit and circulation (`docs/training/chastity.md`). Consult a physician for any concerns about prolonged chastity or anal play.

## Sissygasm (hands-free prostate orgasm)

A sissygasm is orgasm driven primarily or solely by prostate stimulation, usually without penile contact — experienced as deeper, full-body waves rather than localized ejaculatory release, with or without ejaculation.

Progression:

1. **Abstinence or chastity first** — days to weeks of lock time builds tension and dethrones the penis (`docs/training/chastity.md`, `docs/training/denial.md`).
2. **Prepare** — empty bladder and bowels, relax with a warm bath or breathing, plenty of water-based or hybrid lube.
3. **Find the prostate** — a lubricated finger or beginner curved toy 5–8 cm in, on the anterior (belly-button) wall; firm, walnut-sized.
4. **Non-goal sessions** — slow circles, come-hither strokes, rhythmic pressure; never forceful thrusting. Hands-free massagers that respond to pelvic contractions, or suction-cup toys for riding, work well.
5. **Kegels** — contract the pelvic floor to press the toy against the prostate, release, repeat, paired with slow diaphragmatic breathing.
6. **Escalate** — low vibration, longer sessions (20–60+ minutes), positions: on the back with knees bent, prone, or riding.

Mindset decides it: climax chases away. Most users succeed only after they stop trying to make it happen — pair the sessions with erotic imagery, lingerie, or audio that supports a receptive, surrendered state. First success may take several sessions; subsequent ones come easier.

## Plapping

Rhythmic light-to-moderate slapping or tapping of the balls — against the body, a caged penis, or a toy — producing a characteristic sound. In training it is an alternative stimulation and a humiliation-flavored one, often combined with chastity, edging, and degrading framing; with enough buildup it can produce orgasm (a "plapgasm").

Technique: start gentle, intensity is highly individual. Hand slaps, toy strikes, or machine assistance while locked. Pair with prior edging and visual stimulation, and avoid direct penile stroking — some progress from combined stimulation (vibrator on cage plus plapping) to plapping alone. Monitor force; fertility concerns arise mainly with repeated hard impacts.

## Caged vibration

While locked, vibration applied to the cage, base ring, or perineum transmits sensation and supports hands-free or low-touch orgasm. Start low and intermittent; vibrating cages, add-on rings, and wands all work — verify no pinching or circulation compromise. Sessions often end in a leaking, ruined release rather than a conventional one — which is the point (`docs/training/denial.md`).

## Nipple conditioning

Male nipples activate brain regions overlapping genital stimulation; roughly half of men report arousal from nipple play, and a subset can reach nipple-only orgasm with practice.

- Begin indirectly: massage the surrounding chest before touching the nipple.
- Progress through light circling, rolling, gentle pinching and tugging, varying pressure and speed; then suction devices, clamps (loose and brief at first), vibrators.
- **Pleasure transfer**: combine nipple play with established genital or prostate arousal, then gradually shift emphasis to the nipples.
- Dedicated, extended sessions without drifting to the genitals. Consistency across weeks raises responsiveness; multi-zone play (nipples plus prostate or cage) pairs well.

## Removing the stroking instinct

Stroking is a strongly conditioned motor-reward loop; extinction needs barriers plus replacement:

- **Chastity** prevents access and forces sensory redirection (`docs/training/chastity.md`).
- **Immediate redirect**: when arousal rises, go straight to an approved alternative — prostate, nipples, humping (`docs/training/humping.md`), mental fantasy, breathing.
- **Reward pairing**: release happens *only* through the target method — edge via permitted means, finish through prostate, cage vibration, or grinding. The automatic reach-to-stroke fades as the alternative circuits earn the reward.
- Mental support: hypno audio framed on denial and redirection, affirmations, journaling urges. Distraction and structured routines absorb residual urges.

Many report substantial reduction of the impulse after weeks of strict non-stroking paired with rewarding alternatives.

## Limp and small training

Permanent anatomical reduction is not on the table — erectile tissue does not vanish — but disuse and conditioning change function and appearance:

- **Extended chastity** with progressively longer wear and eventual downsizing restricts erections and encourages a smaller flaccid state (`docs/training/chastity.md`).
- **Flaccidity conditioning**: practice staying soft under mild-to-moderate arousal via breathing, progressive relaxation, visualization of limpness, and immediate redirection when an erection starts. Ice can assist fitting or training moments.
- **Mental reinforcement**: limp-focused imagery and audio supporting acceptance and preference for a soft, non-dominant state.
- Tucking or inverted/flat-style cages minimize visible presence further.

Any size change from disuse reverses with resumed erectile activity — the lasting effect is behavioral and perceptual. Frame it that way honestly; the fantasy of permanence stays fantasy (`docs/training/tailoring.md` covers fantasy-versus-endpoint).

## Integrated sessions

Combine elements in one arc: lock, tease, stimulate nipples and prostate, add controlled plapping or humping, keep the mental state receptive. Track progress in reflection — sensations, durations, mental state. Balance recovery periods; sustainability beats obsession.
