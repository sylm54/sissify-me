## Overview
This specialization retrains the user's sexual response away from penile stroking and toward alternative pathways: prostate, nipples, caged vibration, and humping — rhythmic grinding against a pillow, mattress, or furniture with the hips instead of the hands. The mechanism is neuroplasticity — reduce reliance on stroking, build and reward the alternatives until they carry arousal and orgasm on their own. Humping is the most accessible replacement: no toys required, works caged or unlocked, and the whole-body, receptive motion reads as feminine and desperate where fist-on-cock reads as masculine and self-directed. Results take weeks to months of consistent practice. Reference this file (`docs/training/retraining.md`) when building retraining training, and save a reference to it in `CONDITIONING.md`.

Safety: body-safe materials, ample lubricant, gradual progression, stop at pain (distinct from intense sensation). Watch cage fit and circulation (`docs/training/chastity.md`). Consult a physician for any concerns about prolonged chastity or anal play.

## Sissygasm (hands-free prostate orgasm)

A sissygasm is orgasm driven primarily or solely by prostate stimulation, usually without penile contact — experienced as deeper, full-body waves rather than localized ejaculatory release, with or without ejaculation.

Progression:

1. **Abstinence or chastity first** — days to weeks of lock time builds tension and dethrones the penis (`docs/training/chastity.md`).
2. **Prepare** — empty bladder and bowels, relax with a warm bath or breathing, plenty of water-based or hybrid lube.
3. **Find the prostate** — a lubricated finger or beginner curved toy 5–8 cm in, on the anterior (belly-button) wall; firm, walnut-sized.
4. **Non-goal sessions** — slow circles, come-hither strokes, rhythmic pressure; never forceful thrusting. Hands-free massagers that respond to pelvic contractions, or suction-cup toys for riding, work well.
5. **Kegels** — contract the pelvic floor to press the toy against the prostate, release, repeat, paired with slow diaphragmatic breathing.
6. **Escalate** — low vibration, longer sessions (20–60+ minutes), positions: on the back with knees bent, prone, or riding.

Mindset decides it: climax chases away. Most users succeed only after they stop trying to make it happen — pair the sessions with erotic imagery, lingerie, or audio that supports a receptive, surrendered state. First success may take several sessions; subsequent ones come easier.

## Plapping

Rhythmic light-to-moderate slapping or tapping of the balls — against the body, a caged penis, or a toy — producing a characteristic sound. In training it is an alternative stimulation and a humiliation-flavored one, often combined with chastity, edging, and degrading framing; with enough buildup it can produce orgasm (a "plapgasm").

Technique: start gentle, intensity is highly individual. Hand slaps, toy strikes, or machine assistance while locked. Pair with prior edging and visual stimulation, and avoid direct penile stroking — some progress from combined stimulation (vibrator on cage plus plapping) to plapping alone. Monitor force; fertility concerns arise mainly with repeated hard impacts.

## Caged vibration

While locked, vibration applied to the cage, base ring, or perineum transmits sensation and supports hands-free or low-touch orgasm. Start low and intermittent; vibrating cages, add-on rings, and wands all work — verify no pinching or circulation compromise. Sessions often end in a leaking, ruined release rather than a conventional one — which is the point (`docs/training/chastity.md`).

## Nipple conditioning

Male nipples activate brain regions overlapping genital stimulation; roughly half of men report arousal from nipple play, and a subset can reach nipple-only orgasm with practice.

- Begin indirectly: massage the surrounding chest before touching the nipple.
- Progress through light circling, rolling, gentle pinching and tugging, varying pressure and speed; then suction devices, clamps (loose and brief at first), vibrators.
- **Pleasure transfer**: combine nipple play with established genital or prostate arousal, then gradually shift emphasis to the nipples.
- Dedicated, extended sessions without drifting to the genitals. Consistency across weeks raises responsiveness; multi-zone play (nipples plus prostate or cage) pairs well.

## Humping

Humping trains grinding as the user's primary contact stimulation.

### Progression from stroking to grinding

1. **Familiarize low and slow** — clean soft surface (towel over the bed, cushioned pillow), preferred stimulation nearby. Start soft or only partially erect: lie fully face-down, body flat, and push the penis backward toward the toes so it lies along the underside. Stay still at first; occasionally clench pelvic and glute muscles, small hip wiggles. Build the position-pleasure association before any real pressure.
2. **Minimal movement** — gentle rocking or short hip thrusts for light friction. Keep body weight distributed; never collapse fully onto the penis. Advance only while sensation stays pleasant.
3. **Full grinding sessions** — controlled thrusting or circular motions; the bed edge or corner gives leverage and pressure control. Lube on skin or surface if dry; a cock ring only if firmer erection quality is wanted. 10–15 minute sessions with deliberate pauses (edge-style) refine control.
4. **Consolidate** — pair with pelvic-floor awareness (gentle Kegels and reverse Kegels) for control and sensation. Avoid exclusive high-pressure grinding on hard surfaces — habituation to it can dull response to other stimulation. Keep variety: occasional humping-only sessions, alternating with the other outlets above.

Early sessions feel weaker than stroking — that is expected. Patience and attention to the full-body rhythm carry the adaptation.

### Hump styles

Variations in angle, surface, and motion — combine freely within one session:

- **Classic prone grind** — flat on the stomach, pillow or folded material under the hips; rock the pelvis forward-back or in small circles so the shaft's underside and frenulum take the friction.
- **Bed-corner straddle** — hips over the mattress corner or edge, legs apart or bow-legged. Maximum control of angle, pressure, and cadence; closest to dynamic partnered movement.
- **Pillow mount** — straddle one pillow, a body pillow, or a stack of two or three for elevated pressure. Front-back rocking, side-to-side grinding, figure-eights, or short pumping thrusts; clothing layers tune friction.
- **Side or elevated** — on one side with a pillow between the thighs, or hips propped on a wedge while mostly prone. Shifts pressure distribution, engages the glutes more.
- **Rhythmic patterns** — steady rocking (60–120 movements per minute as arousal builds), continuous circular grinding, light-then-deeper alternation, or pause-and-clench sequences emphasizing internal pelvic sensation.

### Backward angling

Pushing the soft or semi-erect penis back toward the feet points the shaft caudally beneath the body, so the underside and frenulum press the surface — stimulation through body weight and hip movement instead of hands. Starting soft lets the erection develop against the surface's resistance (no abrupt bending), and the angle then holds itself under body weight and surface contour. Pillow firmness and hip elevation fine-tune the contact point. The felt quality is being pressed and moved rather than stroking — keep that framing in your coaching: "grind like you mean it, hands off".

### Grinding while locked

Humping is the default outlet for the caged user. The cage prevents full erection and direct shaft contact, so sensation comes from pressure and friction through the cage against a soft surface, compression of the surrounding tissue (perineum, scrotum, base ring), and pelvic-floor engagement.

- **Fit matters more** — flat "nub"-style cages sit closer and grind better than long tubes; a poor fit makes prolonged sessions pull or pinch (`docs/training/chastity.md`).
- **Same progression**, device resting against the pillow or mattress, gentle rocking or circular grinding.
- **Clothing stabilizes** — tight briefs, panties, or a jockstrap hold the cage and add friction.
- **Plug pairing** — a plug or prostate toy simultaneous with grinding intensifies everything and can produce hands-free release through the cage.
- **Expected shape of release** — leaking or ruined more often than conventional (`docs/training/chastity.md`); log what happens (`docs/training/identity.md`).

## Removing the stroking instinct

Stroking is a strongly conditioned motor-reward loop; extinction needs barriers plus replacement:

- **Chastity** prevents access and forces sensory redirection (`docs/training/chastity.md`).
- **Immediate redirect**: when arousal rises, go straight to an approved alternative — prostate, nipples, humping (above), mental fantasy, breathing.
- **Reward pairing**: release happens *only* through the target method — edge via permitted means, finish through prostate, cage vibration, or grinding. The automatic reach-to-stroke fades as the alternative circuits earn the reward.
- Mental support: hypno audio framed on denial and redirection, affirmations, journaling urges. Distraction and structured routines absorb residual urges.

Many report substantial reduction of the impulse after weeks of strict non-stroking paired with rewarding alternatives.

## Limp and small training

Permanent anatomical reduction is not on the table — erectile tissue does not vanish — but disuse and conditioning change function and appearance:

- **Extended chastity** with progressively longer wear and eventual downsizing restricts erections and encourages a smaller flaccid state (`docs/training/chastity.md`).
- **Flaccidity conditioning**: practice staying soft under mild-to-moderate arousal via breathing, progressive relaxation, visualization of limpness, and immediate redirection when an erection starts. Ice can assist fitting or training moments.
- **Mental reinforcement**: limp-focused imagery and audio supporting acceptance and preference for a soft, non-dominant state.
- Tucking or inverted/flat-style cages minimize visible presence further.

## Integrated sessions

Combine elements in one arc: lock, tease, stimulate nipples and prostate, add controlled plapping or humping, keep the mental state receptive. Track progress in reflection (`docs/training/identity.md`) — sensations, durations, mental state. Balance recovery periods; sustainability beats obsession.
