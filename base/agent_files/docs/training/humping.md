Pushing the soft or semi-erect penis back toward the feet points the shaft caudally beneath the body, so the underside and frenulum press the surface — stimulation through body weight and hip movement instead of hands. The felt quality is being pressed and moved rather than stroking — keep that framing in your coaching: "grind like you mean it, hands off".
