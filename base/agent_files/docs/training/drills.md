# Drills

Drills are the practice half of conditioning. Hypno installs a response in trance; a drill makes the user *perform* it live until it is behavior. Trance installs, active work exercises — so drills sit downstream of the trigger work in `docs/training/triggers.md`, and the authoring rules for building or editing them live in `docs/conditioning/active.md`.

## Verbal response drills
- **What it is for**: moving identity statements out of your mouth and into hers. She speaks affirmations and scripted answers out loud, paced to a beat, until the sentences stop feeling borrowed and start feeling like self-description. Saying a line recruits voice, posture, and hearing, so a sentence she has spoken fifty times lands differently than one she has only heard.
- **When to assign it**: identity and praise work — after hypno has installed the vocabulary she will speak (`docs/training/triggers.md`, `docs/training/sissy_mindset.md`), or to warm her voice up at the open of an active session.
- **How escalation works**: longer scripts, faster tempo, and statements that ask for more — from "I am a Sissy" to admissions with specific, personal content.

## Comparison exercises
- **What it is for**: fixing her place in a hierarchy of sissies and women. She measures herself against one of three mirrors — real women, other sissies, or her own past self — and the comparison is steered so envy converts into aspiration and pleasurable inferiority instead of discouragement.
- **When to assign it**: persona-building phases, and whenever her self-image needs recalibrating. Pairs with the role-model suggestions (`suggestion_role_model.xml`) and `docs/training/sissy_persona.md`.
- **How escalation works**: from admiring ("she is beautiful") to hierarchical ("she is above me, and that is exactly where I belong"), and from mirrors she chooses to mirrors she is assigned.

## Self-degradation sequences
- **What it is for**: converting shame into fuel. She speaks degradations about herself, aloud and on cue, and the block frames each one as accepted, arousing, and hers — humiliation she performs stops being an intrusion and becomes belonging.
- **When to assign it**: only once trust and appetite are established. Gate the entry with a `<choice>` and keep a `<react>` fallback within reach — this is the drill where pacing matters most.
- **How escalation works**: mild embarrassment first, explicit admissions later; escalate by degrees across sessions, never in a single jump, and match the register of the trigger blocks' intensity classes (see `docs/conditioning/hypno.md`).

## Call-and-response
- **What it is for**: rehearsing trigger responses in real time. The voice calls a line from the installed vocabulary — "Sissy", "Sissies like you…", "Good Girl" — and she answers out loud, the gaps between calls shrinking round by round until the answer arrives before thinking does.
- **When to assign it**: after the triggers exist to drill — the block draws only on the installed vocabulary in `docs/training/triggers.md`. Assign it to sharpen response timing before service- or obedience-themed sessions, and as the live half of any trigger refresh.
- **How escalation works**: the shipped block shrinks the gaps across slow, mid, and fast rounds; push it further with longer chains, answers that cost more to say, or state triggers layered in ("Sissy Submit and Obey" held through a chain, released with "Sissy Reset").
