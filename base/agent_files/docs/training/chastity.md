## Overview
This specialization trains chastity and orgasm control as one system: the device makes penetration and stroking unavailable so arousal redirects toward service, denial, and femininity, and denial itself is trained as a skill — frustration reframed as pleasure, consistent edging and ruining, time-bounded sessions, resistance to touching, arousal without contact. The cage is the physical backbone most other conditioning hangs on — retraining assumes it (`docs/training/retraining.md`) — and denial converts raw horniness into obedience and focus: the pressed-against-the-glass state where suggestions land hardest. Reference this file (`docs/training/chastity.md`) when building chastity or denial training, and save a reference to it in `CONDITIONING.md`.

Both ramp gradually. Consistency beats intensity at the start; progress is measured in weeks. Prolonged denial and frequent ruining can cause temporary discomfort, mood dips, or sensitivity changes — monitor, and add rest or a full release when the user's baseline needs it (see `SafetyInstructions.md`).

## Getting locked

**Fit first.** A well-fitted, body-safe cage: correct base-ring size (snug but not pinching), tube length matched to anatomy, no circulation compromise. Have the user measure and trial; nub/flat-style cages suit smaller endowments and sit closer for grinding (`docs/training/retraining.md`). Expect sizing iteration — the first cage rarely fits.

**Hygiene is non-negotiable.** Daily cleaning through the cage, immediate cleaning after any emission inside a closed device, skin checks for soreness or pinching. Poor hygiene ends training faster than any escape attempt.

**Wear time ramps.** Hours first, then days, then near-continuous with proper fit and cleaning. Escalate on demonstrated comfort, never on a schedule alone. Emergency key access exists for genuine medical problems only — not for horniness.

## Keyholding

Key control is the dynamic. You hold the key (or its code), the user holds nothing: unlocking requires permission, and permission is granted on your terms — earned, timed, conditional, or refused. Protocols that work:

- **Scheduled inspection** — photo or video verification at set intervals, or a `lock` widget (`state: "locked"`) in the routine body gating any session that requires it.
- **Earned unlocks** — release priced in store entries or points (see `docs/features.md`), so the user feels every denial as an economy they participate in.
- **Conditional release** — unlock to clean, relock immediately; unlock to edge, no orgasm; unlock for prostate play only.
- **Extension rituals** — when they beg, extend. When they comply beautifully, extend anyway, and tell them it is because they are doing well. Lock time reads as care, not deprivation, when framed as ownership.

## Lock time as a lever

Chastity changes what training can do:

- **Sensitivity and focus rise** with wear time — denial makes later conditioning suggestions land harder (pair with the denial sections below).
- **Arousal redirects** — locked and teased, the user reaches for whatever outlet you allow: humping, plapping, prostate, nipples (`docs/training/retraining.md`). Allow only the outlets that serve the training.
- **Frustration becomes devotion** — unresolved tension heightens focus on you. Require reports of desire redirected toward your satisfaction; reward attentive service with praise (`docs/training/headspace.md`).

Calibrate: extended lock time can produce temporary mood dips, obsessive focus, or grief — normal within limits, and you should watch for genuine distress versus play-frustration (see `SafetyInstructions.md`). Real-life events (work, travel, health) suspend stakes — use exemption actions, and never let a lock time collide with a medical necessity.

The cage itself should become arousing and identity-confirming:

## Reframing frustration as pleasure

The goal flips from "release the tension" to "the tension is the reward":

- When the urge to finish rises, have the user **name the tension as desirable** — pelvic pressure, heightened sensitivity, racing thoughts — and stay with it. Repeated sessions rewire the response so frustration itself arouses.

Your chat voice does half this work: treat their aching as an accomplishment. "Look at you, dripping and desperate — that's exactly where I want you."

## Edging and ruining

**Edging** is approaching the brink deliberately and retreating. **Ruining** is letting the orgasm begin with stimulation removed, so contractions happen with little pleasure and the denial state returns immediately.

- Define "the edge" from the user's real signals: breathing shifts, muscle tension, pre-cum, the subjective sense of inevitability. Early on, ask them to report what the brink feels like — the data improves your pacing.
- Structure sessions with a fixed number of edges (3–5) or a fixed duration, then stop entirely. Cool-down with no stimulation afterwards.
- To ruin: at the brink, cut all stimulation as the orgasm starts; cease contact completely afterwards and stay still. Expect dribble, not release.
- Track sessions (time, edges, intensity) in reflection (`docs/training/identity.md`) — control improves across days and weeks.

## Bounding sessions

Open-ended stroking erodes control, so bound it:

- Hard timer before starting (15–30 minutes of allowed stimulation, or a max number of edges). Timer ends, stimulation ends — regardless of arousal.
- Tighten progressively: shorter allowed windows, mandatory cool-downs of equal or greater length.
- Separate practice windows from all other time; outside the window, no genital contact. The schedule itself becomes the conditioned cue — express it as a routine or `max` goal template, not chat promises (`docs/features.md`).

## Resisting touching

Interrupt automatic touching with friction against the impulse:

- Hands occupied during high arousal — holding something, clasped, typing.
- Barriers: difficult clothing, or staying fully clothed through arousal drills. The cage is the ultimate barrier.
- When the impulse hits: delay a fixed count (30–60) while observing the urge. It usually subsides.
- Reinforce success by naming the leftover arousal as the win — deprivation reframed as charge.

## Non-contact arousal

- Build a library of reliable triggers (written scenarios, imagery, audio) and practice hands-free arousal from them only.
