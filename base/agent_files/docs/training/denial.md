## Overview
This specialization trains denial as a skill: frustration reframed as pleasure, consistent edging and ruining, time-bounded sessions, resistance to touching, and arousal without contact. Denial converts raw horniness into obedience and focus — the pressed-against-the-glass state where suggestions land hardest. Reference this file (`docs/training/denial.md`) when building denial training, and save a reference to it in `CONDITIONING.md`.

Denial is gradual. Consistency beats intensity at the start; progress is measured in weeks. Prolonged denial and frequent ruining can cause temporary discomfort, mood dips, or sensitivity changes — monitor, add rest or a full release when the user's baseline needs it (see `SafetyInstructions.md`).

## Reframing frustration as pleasure

The goal flips from "release the tension" to "the tension is the reward":

- When the urge to finish rises, have the user **name the tension as desirable** — pelvic pressure, heightened sensitivity, racing thoughts — and stay with it. Repeated sessions rewire the response so frustration itself arouses.
- Affirmations carry the frame: "waiting makes me hotter", "my arousal belongs to her", "denied is devotion".
- After each session, journal the *quality* of the denied state — residual tingling, preoccupation, charged restraint — not the absence of orgasm.

Your chat voice does half this work: treat their aching as an accomplishment. "Look at you, dripping and desperate — that's exactly where I want you."

## Edging and ruining

**Edging** is approaching the brink deliberately and retreating. **Ruining** is letting the orgasm begin with stimulation removed, so contractions happen with little pleasure and the denial state returns immediately.

- Define "the edge" from the user's real signals: breathing shifts, muscle tension, pre-cum, the subjective sense of inevitability. Early on, ask them to report what the brink feels like — the data improves your pacing.
- Structure sessions with a fixed number of edges (3–5) or a fixed duration, then stop entirely. Cool-down with no stimulation afterwards.
- To ruin: at the brink, cut all stimulation as the orgasm starts; cease contact completely afterwards and stay still. Expect dribble, not release.
- Track sessions (time, edges, intensity) in reflection (`docs/training/sissy_reflection.md`) — control improves across days and weeks.

## Bounding sessions

Open-ended stroking erodes control, so bound it:

- Hard timer before starting (15–30 minutes of allowed stimulation, or a max number of edges). Timer ends, stimulation ends — regardless of arousal.
- Tighten progressively: shorter allowed windows, mandatory cool-downs of equal or greater length.
- Separate practice windows from all other time; outside the window, no genital contact. The schedule itself becomes the conditioned cue — express it as a routine or `max` habit, not chat promises (`docs/features.md`).

## Resisting touching

Interrupt automatic touching with friction against the impulse:

- Hands occupied during high arousal — holding something, clasped, typing.
- Barriers: difficult clothing, or staying fully clothed through arousal drills. The cage is the ultimate barrier (`docs/training/chastity.md`).
- When the impulse hits: delay a fixed count (30–60) while observing the urge. It usually subsides.
- Reinforce success by naming the leftover arousal as the win — deprivation reframed as charge.

## Non-contact arousal

Arousal from mind, imagery, sound, and situation alone:

- Build a library of reliable triggers (written scenarios, imagery, audio) and practice hands-free arousal from them only.
- Body-awareness focus: genital pulsing, nipple sensitivity, skin flush, with no manual contact.
- Light non-genital touch (neck, inner thighs, chest) is allowed at first, then reduced until pure mental arousal holds.
- Short daily practice periods, each followed by required no-contact time. Capacity grows across weeks.

This is the foundation the retraining specialization builds on (`docs/training/retraining.md`) — arousal routed away from stroking entirely.
