## Overview
This is the framework's trigger library: the triggers useful for conditioning. There are no tiers — every trigger here is a working tool, and sessions combine them freely. Reference this file (`docs/training/triggers.md`) when installing triggers, and save a reference to it in `CONDITIONING.md`.

A file may be `<include>`d at most once per render tree — a session installing several triggers includes each block once.

## Installation sequence
Install every trigger — prebuilt block or custom script — in the order **Suggestion → Trigger** (see `docs/conditioning/hypno.md`):
1. **Elicit and intensify** the target state while the subject is in trance, with specific, sensory, present-tense language — before the trigger is ever named.
2. **Install at the peak**: introduce the trigger phrase and link it explicitly ("…and while you are this deep, whenever you hear '—', this exact feeling floods back, instantly, stronger each time").
3. **Reinforce immediately**: restate the pairing in varied words, fire the trigger in trance, and ratify ("you feel it working already, don't you").
4. **Future-pace**: place the trigger where the subject will actually meet the cue again.

Reinforce installed triggers periodically in later sessions — unused triggers fade. The "every time you hear…" sample lines in the sections below are **reinforcement-stage phrasings** for steps 2–3: never open a cold install with the trigger itself; lead with elicitation. Keep all phrasing present-tense and positive — the unconscious handles negation poorly. And keep each trigger's suggestions moving between the specific and the abstract: a concrete behaviour establishes the change, the label organises it into identity — pair them.

## State triggers persist until "Sissy Reset"
Three triggers switch a state **on** that stays on: **Sissy Accept**, **Sissy Brain Off**, and **Sissy Submit and Obey**. Each state persists — through the rest of the session, or into daily life if you future-pace it there — until the subject hears **"Sissy Reset"**, which releases it.

- **"Sissy Reset" is the single universal release.** Install it in the same session that installs any state trigger, so the off switch is reliably conditioned alongside the states.
- **Demonstrate the on/off pairing.** Fire a state, let it settle, fire "Sissy Reset", and ratify the contrast — the state lifts while the conditioning underneath stays ("the state was weather; this is climate"). The pairing in the same session is what makes both halves stick.
- **Reset clears states, never the conditioning.** After a reset the subject finds the same thing underneath: conditioned, feminine, obedient. Temporary states come and go; the conditioning is the authentic baseline.
- **Layer with care.** States combine naturally (Brain Off + Submit and Obey is a classic deep-service stack), but layer two at most before demonstrating the combination works, and keep Reset available.

The other five triggers are one-shot or ambient by design: "Sissy" and "Sissies like you" are identity vocabulary (always active), "Sissy Drop" induces trance, "Good Girl" rewards, and the sight cues fire on seeing.

## Sissy
Primary identity anchor. Upon hearing or reading the word "Sissy", the subject experiences an immediate, automatic recognition of their true nature as a sissy. Critical thought softens; receptivity increases. The word itself becomes a shorthand for obedience, femininity, and submission.

### Implementation
- **Name**: "Sissy" becomes a name they associate with themselves — the way "Lady" names a woman or "Doctor" names a doctor, the word simply refers to them. "You are a Sissy. Sissies like you know that 'Sissy' is a name for them — whenever you hear or read the word, you know it means you."
- **Identity Reinforcement**: Use the word frequently in hypno sessions, training scripts, and daily interactions. "Every time you hear 'Sissy', you feel a wave of feminine energy wash over you, making you feel more and more like the sissy you are meant to be."
- **Emotional Association**: Pair the word with positive, arousing sensations — submission, excitement at feminization, pride. "When you hear 'Sissy', you feel a rush of pleasure and pride, knowing that you are embracing your true self as a submissive sissy."
- **Behavioral Conditioning**: Use the trigger to elicit specific sissy behaviors and mindsets — increased obedience, eagerness to please, heightened arousal at the thought of being a sissy. "Every time you hear 'Sissy', you find yourself becoming more obedient, more eager to please, and more aroused at the thought of being a sissy."

## Sissies like you [want / have / do …]
Comparative identity suggestion. Statements framed as "Sissies like you want…", "Sissies like you have…", or "Sissies like you do…" are accepted as natural and already true of the subject. Resistance is bypassed through social and identity alignment: the statement arrives pre-agreed by everyone like them.

### Implementation
- **Installation**: The persuasion runs through belonging — if someone says "sissies like you do X", they have noticed it in you, because they know you are a Sissy. So even if the compulsion isn't conscious in the moment, it is simply true of you. "When you hear 'Sissies like you', you know it is true about you — other sissies have this trait, and you are one of them. So even if you don't feel it in the moment, you know it is already yours, because you are a Sissy."
- **Reinforcement**: Use the form often, with varied completions, in hypno sessions and training scripts — and inside the suggestion pools themselves. "Every time you hear 'Sissies like you', you start to notice that need in yourself — Sissies like you want it, so you want it. Sissies like you do it, so you do it."
- **Emotional Association**: Pair the form with acceptance, belonging, and pride — having the trait is a natural, desirable part of being a Sissy. "When you hear 'Sissies like you', you feel a warm sense of belonging: this is something Sissies like you have, and you are proud to have it too."

## Sissy Drop
Trance-induction trigger. Upon hearing "Sissy Drop" the subject rapidly falls into a deep, receptive hypnotic state — thoughts slow, the body relaxes, awareness narrows — so subsequent suggestions land with heightened intensity and lasting effect. Each drop is faster and deeper than the last.

### Implementation
- **Hypno Trigger**: Use "Sissy Drop" as the consistent cue for entering and re-entering trance in sessions. "Every time you hear 'Sissy Drop', you drop — instantly, deeper every time — into a soft, obedient trance, wide open to the programming that makes you a better sissy."
- **Supporting position in inductions**: It is fine — and effective — to fire "Sissy Drop" as a *supporting* deepening beat at the end of an induction. The induction's own guidance is still the main work that takes the listener under; the trigger reinforces it. Don't avoid it out of a concern that an induction "isn't allowed" to lean on one, and don't let it replace the guidance either. The shipped induction and deepening pools all close with a supporting drop.
- **Scope**: Fire it from induction, deepening, and redeepening content — not from the middle of suggestion pools, where a drop adds nothing the phase doesn't already have.
- **Behavioral Conditioning**: Pair the drop with the sweetness of letting go. "When you hear 'Sissy Drop', you feel that lovely gravity take hold and you simply let go — no resistance, no delay — down into soft, receptive, programmable trance."

## Good Girl / Good Sissy
Reward and reinforcement trigger. Hearing "Good Girl" (or "Good Sissy") produces warm happiness, euphoria, pleasure, and pride. It strengthens the association between desired sissy behaviors and positive emotional payoff, making future compliance more automatic and rewarding.

### Implementation
- **Positive Reinforcement**: Use it as the consistent praise in hypno sessions, training scripts, and daily interactions — every completed task, every obeyed instruction, every honest answer earns it. Fire it right after suggestions are accepted to praise-anchor them. "Every time you hear 'Good Girl', you feel a rush of pleasure and validation, and being good becomes something you want to do again and again."
- **Emotional Association**: Pair the praise with submission, pride, and happiness, so being a "Good Girl" is a rewarding state of being, not just a compliment. "When you hear 'Good Girl', a wave of warmth and pride washes over you — Sissies like you know that being a Good Girl is the best feeling there is, and you chase that praise with everything you do."

## Sissy Accept
Deep-acceptance state. Upon hearing "Sissy Accept", analytical resistance and critical engagement drop instantly, and the state stays on: everything the subject hears while it is active is absorbed with minimal filtering, settling deeply and permanently into the self-concept. Doubt, debate, and the need to verify simply switch off — acceptance feels effortless and pleasurable.

### Implementation
- **Persistence**: The acceptance stays switched on until "Sissy Reset" releases it. "Sissy Accept stays with you — acceptance flowing through everything you hear — until you hear 'Sissy Reset'. Until then, it simply stays."
- **Reinforcement in combination**: Weave the other triggers through it. "Every time you hear 'Sissy Accept', your questions fall silent and you take in whatever follows without thinking or resistance. Good Girls accept. Sissies accept. You accept."
- **Imagery**: Walk the subject through the shift — the noise of debate, then the quiet. "Imagine your mind full of questions and protests. Now you hear 'Sissy Accept'. The noise fades. Your doubts go quiet. Whatever comes next, you simply accept — and it feels good to accept. Accepting is easy. Accepting is natural. Accepting is what a good sissy does."
- **Use**: Fire it before dense or identity-level suggestion passages so they sink in unfiltered — and demonstrate the release ("Sissy Reset") so the on/off pairing is conditioned.

## Sissy Brain Off
Cognitive-silencing state. Upon hearing "Sissy Brain Off", internal dialogue, overthinking, and rational counter-arguments quiet down and stay quiet. The mind becomes open and fully receptive so that suggestions install with maximum efficiency and minimal interference. Where Sissy Accept removes resistance to what is said, Sissy Brain Off removes the chatter itself.

### Implementation
- **Persistence**: The quiet stays until "Sissy Reset" releases it. "Sissy Brain Off stays with you — the mind open and humming-quiet — until you hear 'Sissy Reset'. Until then, all that is left is my voice, and room for whatever I put in it."
- **Reinforcement in combination**: "Every time you hear 'Sissy Brain Off', the thinking winds down like a toy running out of spring — quieter, and quieter, and quiet. Sissies like you think less and feel more. Good Girls listen. Sissies listen. You listen."
- **Imagery**: Guide the subject through the wind-down with one coherent image family (a switch, a screen dimming, thoughts settling like snow). "You hear 'Sissy Brain Off'. The screens in your mind go dark, one by one. No thoughts to finish. No arguments to run. Just a warm, humming quiet — and how good it feels to let it be quiet."
- **Use**: Fire it when the session needs maximum absorption, and demonstrate the release with "Sissy Reset" so the pairing sticks.

## Sissy Reset
State-clearing and reality-reinforcement trigger. Upon hearing "Sissy Reset", any active state — acceptance, the quiet of Brain Off, obedience mode, plus temporary induced states like freeze, limpness, blankness, or heaviness — lifts instantly, and the conviction settles in that the underlying conditioning has always been the subject's authentic reality. The subject returns to a clean, conditioned baseline, ready for further programming. The crucial nuance: it clears the temporary **states**, never the conditioning itself — what remains after a reset is the real her.

### Implementation
- **Reinforcement**: "Every time you hear 'Sissy Reset', the surface washes clean — acceptance lifts, the quiet releases, obedience mode switches off, any haze clears — and underneath, the same thing every time: the conditioning, still there, still true, as if it had always been part of you. Because it has."
- **Imagery**: Build the contrast first (a heavy or blank state), then the clearing. "Feel how blank and heavy you are right now. Now you hear 'Sissy Reset'. A bright, clean wave rolls through you from crown to heels. The heaviness lifts, the blankness dissolves — and what is left is not emptiness. It is you: awake, refreshed, feminine, conditioned. Everything I built is still exactly where it belongs."
- **Consistency**: Use it between contrasting blocks in a session and as the universal release for the state triggers, so the pairing "temporary state off → authentic conditioning stays" is demonstrated live, every time. "Temporary states come and go. The conditioning stays. 'Sissy Reset' shows you the difference, every single time."

## Sissy Submit and Obey

Upon hearing "Sissy Submit and Obey", the subject shifts into compliant, service-oriented mode and stays there until "Sissy Reset" ends it: a natural, pleasant urge to yield control, follow instructions, and prioritize the will of others — especially dominant partners, and the conditioning itself. Connect the abstract shift to concrete behaviour: the lowered gaze, the "yes" arriving before the question finishes, the body positioning itself to serve.

### Implementation
- **Persistence**: The mode stays on until "Sissy Reset" releases it. "Sissy Submit and Obey stays with you — warm, eager, ready to serve — until you hear 'Sissy Reset'. Until then, every instruction meets a happy yes."
- **Reinforcement in combination**: "Every time you hear 'Sissy Submit and Obey', the urge to yield rises warm and pleasant, and your body already knows what to do: eyes down, knees ready, yes before the question ends. Good Girls submit and obey. Sissies submit and obey. Sissies like you submit and obey — so you do."

Your shoulders soften. Every instruction that arrives, you meet with a happy, eager yes, and following it feels like the most natural thing you have done all day."
- **Use**: Fire it to open service- and obedience-focused segments, hand it to daily life where wanted, and end it with "Sissy Reset" whenever a clean slate is the goal.

## Cues (automatic conditioned responses)
Cue blocks condition a **sight**, not a spoken command word: the response fires automatically upon seeing the cue, without anyone saying anything. Install them with the same Suggestion → Trigger order — elicit the response in trance first, then condition the sight itself as the cue at the peak, reinforce, and future-pace to where the subject actually encounters it. Each cue grows stronger with repeated real-world exposure, so pair the blocks with the viewing practices in `docs/training/autogynephilic.md` and `docs/training/cocklust.md`.

The screen stops being something they watch and becomes somewhere they are. "The moment you see a cock, arousal blooms and your mouth waters with the urge to serve — reflexive, instant, and stronger every time."

## Using triggers together
The triggers are a vocabulary, not a menu of isolated effects — sessions speak it fluently:

- **Fire "Good Girl" right after accepted suggestions** to praise-anchor them (the shipped suggestion pools do this).
- **Use "Sissies like you …" constructions inside suggestion lines** so traits arrive pre-agreed by everyone like her.
- **Address her as "Sissy"** — the name trigger makes every use a reinforcement.
- **Fire "Sissy Accept" / "Sissy Brain Off" before the heaviest material**, and release with "Sissy Reset" when the phase ends.
- **Redeepen between blocks with "Sissy Drop"** (induction/deepening/redeepening content, or a supporting beat — never mid-pool).
- **Pair cues with practice**: the sight cues compound with the viewing habits in `docs/training/autogynephilic.md` and `docs/training/cocklust.md`.
