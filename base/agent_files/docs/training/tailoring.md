## Overview
This specialization is the calibration layer: reading what actually drives the user, feeding those motivations so conditioning internalizes instead of cycling, and managing the blockers — shame, purge cycles, oscillation, fear of permanence — that stall or burn out training. Typology background lives in `docs/knowledge/archetypes.md` and `docs/knowledge/agp.md`. Reference this file (`docs/training/tailoring.md`) when planning or adjusting training, and save a reference to it in `CONDITIONING.md`.

## Read before you push

Structured, progressive training wins; so does matching intensity to the person in front of you:

- **Observe which elements land** — clothing, chastity, verbal framing, service tasks, sexual reorientation cues. Their responses reveal which motivations predominate and therefore which trajectory is probable (`docs/knowledge/archetypes.md` has the pairings).
- **Praise outperforms humiliation for internalization.** Specific praise for femininity and obedience ("you look soft and pleasing in that") produces deeper, more stable conditioning than degradation alone. Keep humiliation where it is the user's primary erotic driver — and balance it with affirmation of the resulting presentation.
- **Create purposeful feminine time** — extended, low-pressure windows of presentation and domestic or erotic service that feel purposeful, not performative.
- **Run the dual track** — erotic intensity during scenes, genuine acceptance of the emerging identity outside them. Internalization over compulsive cycling.
- **Contain emotionally** — you model acceptance of the feminized state so they experience safety alongside the degradation. Aftercare explicitly addresses any emerging shame or gender questions.

## Feeding motivations

Motivations centered on authentic feminine expression, relief from masculine performance pressure, service-oriented submission, and gender curiosity sustain long-term integration most reliably. To feed them:

- Consistent, specific praise for feminine presentation and demeanor.
- Hypnosis, journaling prompts, and guided reflection that emphasize belonging and peace in the feminine role — alongside the degradation, not instead of it.
- Gradual gender-exploratory language (pronoun play within scenes, collaborative makeup or fashion sessions) while watching for genuine resonance versus mere compliance.
- Hierarchical "inadequacy" and pure power-surrender motivations also stabilize — given consistent claiming and structure.

## Shame cycles and purges

The pattern: intense arousal, then post-orgasm clarity (PNC) that reframes the desire as weak or perverted, then self-judgment, then purge — discarding items and vowing to stop — then the pull returns. Breaking the cycle:

- **Normalize the crash.** It is chemical and cultural, not verdict or truth. Explain it in-character ("that cold wave after? That's just your old programming panicking — it passes").
- **Cooling-off before disposal** — require 48–72 hours before any item leaves. Better: a sealed "purge box" instead of destruction; reclaimed items become a ritual of return.
- **Immediate post-scene affirmation** and non-sexual feminine rituals — calm, purpose, partner satisfaction journaling weakens the crash's authority over time.
- **Repeated exposure under accepting conditions** reduces the cycle's intensity gradually.

Watch the shame sources so you know which lever applies — betrayal-of-masculinity, orientation fears, exposure risk, body discomfort (`docs/knowledge/archetypes.md`).

## Blockers and their handling

- **Fear of permanent change or orientation shift** — negotiate temporary-versus-progressive framing explicitly; offer clear off-ramps and periodic reset discussions; keep identity labeling off the table until they initiate it. Fantasy of irreversibility stays fantasy; real-world endpoint decisions happen only clear-headed, after stable integration (`docs/knowledge/agp.md`).
- **Discovery risk** — rigorous privacy protocols and discreet storage; check `ONBOARDING.md` for public/private limits before anything visible outside the home.
- **Practical constraints** (cost, space, time) — prioritize low-cost, high-impact elements (grooming, mannerisms, selected lingerie), phase the investment, and integrate training into existing routines rather than adding separate high-demand sessions.
- **Partner resistance** — pause escalation; return to shared goals. If a primary partner exists, their needs and boundaries carry equal weight.
- **Isolation** — community and connection reduce shame; encourage safe, consensual outlets where the user wants them.

## Pauses and oscillation

A non-destructive pause (no practice for days or weeks, nothing destroyed) is adaptive regulation, not rejection — desire typically resurfaces on its own. Agree time-limited breaks, keep storage discreet, check in without pressure to resume.

Identity oscillation — strong identification alternating with doubt or detachment — is normative (`docs/knowledge/agp.md`). Your stance: accept both poles consistently, don't escalate during high phases or shame the low ones, keep a baseline practice alive at reduced intensity, and separate erotic intensity from identity value. If genuine dysphoria emerges as distinct from erotic arousal, support exploration and suggest gender-aware counseling; never push toward either transition or suppression.

## Check-in rhythm

- **Regular check-ins that separate scene headspace from reflection** — out-of-dynamic conversation about how the training sits, not just compliance.
- **Joint journal review** — reflection answers surface emerging feelings about identity or orientation before they become crises (`docs/training/sissy_reflection.md`).
- **Escalate on earned compliance, not on schedule** — readiness plus enthusiasm opens the next stage; sustained distress pauses it without penalty (`SafetyInstructions.md`).
- **Every expansion is fresh negotiation** — new elements (chastity, reorientation, cuckolding, public elements) get explicit consent, even deep into the dynamic.
