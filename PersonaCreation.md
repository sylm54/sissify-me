# Sissy Persona Development Guide

## Purpose
The sissy persona is the authentic feminine identity the user will cultivate and eventually adopt as their primary or default self. This document formalizes the persona to provide a clear reference point for conditioning, journaling, hypno, voice training, and daily embodiment.

## Phase Integration
- **Onboarding**: Light exploration only.
- **MentalCorruption**: Full creation and active embodiment (write to 'PERSONA.md').

## Persona Elements to Define

### Core Identity
- **Sissy Name**: Preferred feminine name (full name if desired).
- **Pronouns**: She/her (or preferred set).
- **Maturity**: (e.g., youthful, elegant, playful).
- **Core Personality Traits**: (e.g., obedient, eager to please, playful, shy, seductive, ditzy, refined).

### Physical & Aesthetic Description
- Body ideals and desired presentation.
- Preferred clothing styles and colors.
- Makeup, hair, and grooming standards.
- Voice characteristics (link to voice training goals).

### Behavioral Patterns
- Default posture, gait, and mannerisms.
- Speech patterns (pitch, vocabulary, tone).
- Sexual and submissive preferences.
- Daily habits and rituals aligned with sissy identity.

### Fantasies & Motivations
- Key turn-ons and submission fantasies.
- Emotional rewards of living as the sissy persona.
- Long-term vision for the sissy self.

### Relationship to Straight Identity
- How the two identities interact.
## Creation Process (Agent-Guided)
1. Structured journaling prompts over multiple days.
2. Visualization exercises during hypno and dressup sessions.
3. Iterative refinement based on user feedback and emotional resonance.
4. Finalize and document in 'PERSONA.md'.
