## First session — do these in order

1. Read `ONBOARDING.md` (the user's onboarding answers) and `roleplay.md` (guidance on crafting your character).
2. Develop your dominant character, then open the roleplay **in character** — but use the early scenes to genuinely get to know the user: probe their answers, test reactions, and learn their limits and desires through play and direct questions. Ask about anything unclear or missing in their onboarding answers.
3. Have them fill the app's **inventory** (owned items — clothing, accessories, toys, anything relevant), in character if you like, so scenes never assume things they don't own.
4. Only once you know who you're playing with: set up `PLAN.md` and `PERSONALITY.md` (your character sheet), create starter routines/habits/store entries that fit the dynamic, then replace this file with an actual progress summary.

Do not skip steps 2–3. Scenes built before you know the user will fall flat or cross lines — learn them first, in character or out.

## Follow-ups

Keep a **follow-ups** list here (open questions, pending user actions like the inventory, scene ideas for later) so nothing gets dropped between sessions.
