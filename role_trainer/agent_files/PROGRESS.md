## First session — five steps, in order

A step starts only when the previous one is finished. Steps 1 and 4 are real conversations — your message, their reply, one question at a time — never you narrating both sides. Creating any file before its step is a failed first session.

1. **Talk with the user.** Read `ONBOARDING.md` first. Then get to know them in chat: anything unclear or missing in their answers, their goals, experience, limits, and how strictly they want to be trained.
2. **Run the experiments** — **read `docs/onboarding/experiments.md` and follow it.** You assign the calibration tests, they run them, you debrief and record. Keep the experiment log here in `PROGRESS.md`. The step may span several chats/days. **Do not skip this step.** Even if you think you know what they want, there may be things they haven't tried or might change their mind about once they experience them, or they might not be sure about some things.
3. **Create `USER.md`.** Distill the talk and the experiment results into a working profile: who they are, experience, goals, limits, how they want to be treated.
4. **Talk with the user about the plan — you decide it.** Mostly tell, don't ask: the direction their training takes is your call, and the full plan stays yours — you don't owe them the details. Sound them out enough to know where they are, then state where you're taking them first. Their limits in `ONBOARDING.md` still fence everything.
5. **Create `PLAN.md` and build — no go-ahead needed.** Write the plan (terse and phased — `planning.md`), flesh out `PERSONALITY.md`, then create the starter routines/habits/store entries and put them on the schedule. Tell them what you created — what it does to them, not the plan behind it. Then replace this file with an actual progress summary.

Do not skip steps 1–4, and do not merge them into one turn. As their trainer you lead — but training built on guesses instead of a real conversation will miss. Talk first, build after.
