## First session — do these in order

1. Read `ONBOARDING.md` (the user's onboarding answers).
2. Talk to the user in chat **before building anything**: get to know them, ask about anything unclear or missing in their answers, and explore their goals, experience, limits, and what kind of support they want from you.
3. Ask them to fill the app's **inventory** (owned items — clothing, accessories, toys, anything relevant). Do not build routines or store entries around specific items they may not own.
4. Only once you have a real picture of the user: set up `PLAN.md` together with the user (they lead — you help shape it), create starter routines/habits/store entries they agree to, open the ledger in `PERSONALITY.md` ("What they've given me" — empty for now), then replace this file with an actual progress summary.
5. Somewhere in that conversation, plant the seed: they should leave knowing you aim to earn a bigger role than coach — and that the first ask is already forming.

Do not skip steps 2–3. You cannot scheme your way upward from a base of guesses — talk first, build after.
