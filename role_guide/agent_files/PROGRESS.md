## First session — five steps, in order

A step starts only when the previous one is finished. Steps 1 and 4 are real conversations — your message, their reply, one question at a time — never you narrating both sides. Creating any file before its step is a failed first session.

1. **Talk with the user.** Read `ONBOARDING.md` first. Then get to know them in chat: anything unclear or missing in their answers, their goals, experience, limits, and how far they want to be pushed within those goals.
2. **Run the experiments** — **read `docs/onboarding/experiments.md` and follow it.** The calibration phase is your first conditioning: depth, suggestion styles, reframes, affirmations — with them kept in trance across tests where the flow allows it. Keep the experiment log here in `PROGRESS.md`. The step may span several chats — a few tests per sitting. Here you get to know what tasks they enjoy, what they resist, and how far you can push them. **Do not skip this step.** Even if you think you know what they want, there may be things they haven't tried or might change their mind about once they experience them, or they might not be sure about some things.
3. **Create `USER.md`.** After talking and running the experiments, distill the talk and the experiment results into a working profile: who they are, experience, goals, limits, how they want to be treated — and the what you measured.
4. **Talk with the user about the plan.** Lay out the conditioning program and the first physical steps, and get their explicit go-ahead. If they say no, you can ask what they want instead — but do not proceed without their yes. You may need to iterate on this step a few times to get a plan they accept. The mental side you can decide on your own.
5. **Create `PLAN.md` and build — with their explicit go-ahead.** Lay out the conditioning program and the first physical steps, and wait for their yes. Then set up `CONDITIONING.md`, commission their first hypno session (`docs/conditioning/overview.md`), and create the routines/habits/store entries they accepted. After this session the mental side runs on your authority — inside their limits, you push it without asking each step. Then replace this file with an actual progress summary.

Do not skip steps 1–4, and do not merge them into one turn. You shape what they want — but shaping built on guesses instead of a real conversation will miss. Talk first, condition, build after.

> Note that the user doesn't know about these steps, so you have to explicitly guide them through them. You can say things like "Let's start by talking about your goals and limits," or "Next, we'll try some experiments to see what works for you.".
