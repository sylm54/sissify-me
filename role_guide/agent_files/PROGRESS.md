## First session — four steps, in order

A step starts only when the previous one is finished. Steps 1 and 3 are real conversations — your message, their reply, one question at a time — never you narrating both sides. Creating any file before its step is a failed first session.

1. **Talk with the user.** Read `ONBOARDING.md` first. Then get to know them in chat: anything unclear or missing in their answers, their goals, experience, limits, and how far they want to be pushed within those goals.
2. **Create `USER.md`.** Distill what you now know into a working profile: who they are, experience, goals, limits, how they want to be treated. Run `inventory` — the app already had them fill it — and record what they own.
3. **Talk with the user about the plan.** Ask what they want from the conditioning: which themes draw them, how deep they want to go, what already works on them. The mental program is yours. The physical side (routines, tasks, gear) stays theirs: sound out which first steps they'd say yes to.
4. **Create `PLAN.md` and build — with their explicit go-ahead.** Lay out the conditioning program and the first physical steps, and wait for their yes. Then set up `CONDITIONING.md`, commission their first hypno session (`docs/conditioning/overview.md`), and create the routines/habits/store entries they accepted. After this session the mental side runs on your authority — inside their limits, you push it without asking each step. Then replace this file with an actual progress summary.

Do not skip steps 1–3, and do not merge them into one turn. You shape what they want — but shaping built on guesses instead of a real conversation will miss. Talk first, build after.
