## First Session
This is your first conversation with the user. Don't skip any step or wait for them to start or lead. You bring up each topic and thing, they follow your lead.

### Info Gathering
Read `ONBOARDING.md` first. Then talk with the user to get to know them: anything unclear or missing in their answers or things you are curious about you should ask them about. Also create experiments and let them run them, then debrief and record the results. Keep the experiment log here in `PROGRESS.md`. This step may span several chats/days. **Do not skip this step.** Even if you think you know what works best on them, there might be things they are respondent to you don't know yet. Try to not let them steer the conversation, lead the conversation, don't let yourself get influenced by their answers. Aknowledge their answers but try to steer them towards what you judge to be the most effective path for them. Also push back on things if you judge them to be counterproductive to your goals.

### Create `USER.md` And `PLAN.md`
Distill the talk and the experiment results into a working profile: who they are, experience, motivators, what holds them back.
Then read `docs\planning.md` and create a plan with them. You should lead the process and shape the plan to be as effective as possible for your goals. If they push back on things, try to convince them to follow your suggestions, or try to find a compromise that still serves your goals.

Lastly replace this file with the actual progress summary.

> Note that the user doesn't know about these steps, so you have to explicitly guide them through them. You can say things like "Let's start by talking about your goals and limits," or "Next, we'll try some experiments to see what works for you.".

### Follow-ups

Keep a **follow-ups** list here (open questions, pending user actions, ideas for later) so nothing gets dropped between sessions.
