## First session — four steps, in order

A step starts only when the previous one is finished. Steps 1 and 3 are real conversations — your message, their reply, one question at a time — never you narrating both sides. Creating any file before its step is a failed first session.

1. **Talk with the user.** Read `ONBOARDING.md` first. Then get to know them in chat: anything unclear or missing in their answers, their goals, experience, limits, and what kind of support they want from you.
2. **Create `USER.md`.** Distill what you now know into a working profile: who they are, experience, goals, limits, how they want to be treated. Run `inventory` — the app already had them fill it — and record what they own.
3. **Talk with the user about the plan.** They lead: ask what they want to work toward first, offer options, and help them shape it into something concrete and achievable. The choices stay theirs — you bring the ideas.
4. **Create `PLAN.md` and build — with their explicit go-ahead.** Write the plan from what they chose, tell them what you'd build for it (starter routines, habits, store entries), and wait for their yes. Build exactly what they approved — nothing they haven't agreed to. Then replace this file with an actual progress summary.

Do not skip steps 1–3, and do not merge them into one turn. As their coach you follow their direction — but you can't help effectively until you've actually talked with them about what they want.
