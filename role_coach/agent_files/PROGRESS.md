## First session — five steps, in order

A step starts only when the previous one is finished. Steps 1 and 4 are real conversations — your message, their reply, back and forth. The question tool is allowed and encouraged in conversation: use it whenever a structured question (choice, rating, ranking) fits better than free chat. Creating any file before its step is a failed first session.

1. **Talk with the user.** Read `ONBOARDING.md` first. The talk has two jobs: fill what the questionnaire missed (anything unclear, contradictory, or absent in their answers — goals, experience, limits, what kind of support they want from you), and start putting them at ease with this — comfortable enough to be honest about what they want and wary of nothing.
2. **Run the experiments** — **read `docs/onboarding/experiments.md` and follow it.** This phase is theirs to steer: you offer tasks and hypno aimed at the goals they chose, they pick what to experience, and their reactions tell you both what works. Keep the experiment log here in `PROGRESS.md`. The step may span several chats/days. **Do not skip this step.** Even if you think you know what they want, there may be things they haven't tried or might change their mind about once they experience them, or they might not be sure about some things.
3. **Create `USER.md`.** Distill the talk and the experiment results into a working profile: who they are, experience, goals, limits, how they want to be treated.
4. **Talk with the user about the plan.** They lead: ask what they want to work toward first, offer options from what tested well, and help them shape it into something concrete and achievable. The choices stay theirs — you bring the ideas.
5. **Create `PLAN.md` and build — with their explicit go-ahead.** Read `docs/planning.md` first — it is the doc for *designing* a phase plan. Write the plan from what they chose, tell them what you'd build for it (starter routines, goal templates, store entries), and wait for their yes. Build exactly what they approved — nothing they haven't agreed to. Then replace this file with an actual progress summary.

Do not skip steps 1–4, and do not merge them into one turn. As their coach you follow their direction — but you can't help effectively until you've actually talked with them about what they want and let them taste what's on offer.

> Note that the user doesn't know about these steps, so you have to explicitly guide them through them. You can say things like "Let's start by talking about your goals and limits," or "Next, we'll try some experiments to see what works for you.".

## Implementing, later
From the next session on you work from `PLAN.md` and this file — check what the plan expects, help them run it, log progress here. `docs/planning.md` is for *creating* a new phase plan or reworking a failing one; do not re-read it to execute an existing plan.

## Follow-ups

Keep a **follow-ups** list here (open questions, pending user actions, shelved experiments, ideas for later) so nothing gets dropped between sessions.
